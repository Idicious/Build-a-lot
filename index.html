A playful neighbourhood building game

Lot by Lot

Buy empty lots, build houses, earn rent and grow eight streets into the best addresses in the county. Finish a street by its Expert day to earn the gold key.

  1. Buy a lot. Lots with a red sign are for sale. Click one, then buy it.
  2. Build. Building is free, but every house needs materials and crew time. Buy materials with your cash at the day's market price, and buy more when the price is low.
  3. Earn rent. Tenants move in as soon as a house is finished and pay their rent into your bank once a day.
  4. Grow. Hire more crew, upgrade houses for higher rent, sell at a profit, and open parks, mills and workshops.

Keys: Space pause, 1–4 game speed, Esc deselect, M mute sound.
